## 🎓 Understanding the Algorithm

### The IQR Method (Simple)

```
Historical Data:     [10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40]
                       ↑              ↑     ↑
                       Q1             Q2    Q3
                      (15)          (25)   (35)

IQR = 35 - 15 = 20

Outlier Bounds:
  Lower = 15 - (1.5 × 20) = -15  ◄─ Anything below
  Upper = 35 + (1.5 × 20) = 65   ◄─ Anything above

New Data Point: 70
  70 > 65? YES → OUTLIER ✓
  Severity = |70-25| / 10 = 4.5/10
```
